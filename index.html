Hearth: Home Inventory

Know what you own, when it needs attention, and whether it has been recalled.

Hearth helps you keep a record of the appliances and equipment in your home and the maintenance they need. Photograph a nameplate and Hearth reads the label, files the item under a category and brand, and builds a maintenance schedule with reminders you control.

Hearth also checks your saved items against the US Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) recall database and warns you when a brand and category you own turns up in a recall notice.

What it does

  • Add an appliance by photographing its nameplate — category and brand are filled in for you.
  • Keep the details that matter: model, serial number, room, purchase date, price, notes, UPC.
  • Automatic maintenance schedules with reminders you can change, silence, or delete.
  • Repair logs, so you know what has already been serviced.
  • Best-effort recall matching against public CPSC data, with a link to the official notice.
  • Free. No advertising, no in-app purchases, no subscription.
