Hearth Home Inventory

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 21, 2026
Applies to the Hearth: Home Inventory iOS app (bundle ID com.cyan0914.hearth).

1. Overview

Hearth: Home Inventory ("Hearth", "the app") is an iPhone app for keeping a record of the appliances and equipment in your home, scheduling their maintenance, and checking your saved items against public product recall data. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, where it is stored, and how you can delete it.

Hearth is built and operated by an individual developer, not a company. There is one backend service, described in section 4, and no advertising, analytics, or tracking partners of any kind.

By using the app you agree to this policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the app.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account information (Sign in with Apple)

  • Sign in with Apple is the only way to sign in. Hearth has no passwords and supports no other identity provider.
  • From Apple we receive and store the Apple subject identifier for your account, and the email address Apple returns. If you choose to hide your email address, the address Apple returns is a private relay address of the form xxx@privaterelay.appleid.com. We use that address only to contact you about your account.
  • We store an Apple refresh token, encrypted at rest. It exists for exactly one purpose: to revoke your Apple sign-in grant if you delete your Hearth account. It is not used for anything else.

2.2 Items you add

When you add an appliance or piece of equipment, we store the details you enter or confirm: name, category, brand, model, serial number, room or location, purchase date, price, notes, and UPC. Whatever you type is what we store.

2.3 Photos

Photos of nameplates and appliances are uploaded directly from your device to Cloudflare R2 object storage, operated by Cloudflare, Inc. (United States). Our API server never receives the image bytes: it issues a presigned upload URL, and afterwards stores the object key that results. Your photos are stored under a per-user prefix, so one user's objects are kept separate from another's.

2.4 Maintenance tasks and repair logs

The maintenance schedules the app creates for your items, the reminders attached to them, and the repair records you enter.

2.5 Push notification tokens

If you allow notifications, we store the device push token issued by Apple Push Notification service (APNs) so that maintenance reminders can be delivered to your device. The token is deleted when you sign out.

2.6 Session token on your device

A session token is stored on your device in the iOS Keychain to keep you signed in. It is not stored anywhere else.

3. How We Use Information

  • To run the app: store your items, photos, tasks, and logs, and display them back to you.
  • To deliver maintenance: generate schedules and send the reminders you have enabled.
  • To check recalls: match your saved items against public recall data on our server (see section 7).
  • To operate your account: sign-in, session management, and account deletion with Apple grant revocation.
  • To help you: respond when you email support.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or tracking.

4. Where Data Is Stored and Who Processes It

The backend is a Python (FastAPI) service running on a Tencent Cloud server located in Shanghai, China, reached through a Cloudflare tunnel at hearth.taomindapp.com.

  • Tencent Cloud — servers in Shanghai, China, running our API. Your account record, item records, maintenance tasks, repair logs, push tokens, and the encrypted Apple refresh token are stored here.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. — Cloudflare R2 object storage (United States) holds your photos. Cloudflare also provides the tunnel that routes requests to our API.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, APNs for notifications, and the App Store.

These are the only third parties that process your data, and they process it on our behalf to provide the service. We do not sell your data and we do not share it with anyone else.

If you are located outside China, note that account data is transferred to and stored on servers in China, and photos are stored by Cloudflare in the United States.

5. What We Do Not Collect

  • No advertising identifier and no IDFA.
  • No third-party analytics SDK.
  • No crash-reporting SDK.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts.
  • No health data.
  • No payment data — purchases are handled entirely by Apple, and we never receive your card number, billing address, or any other payment detail (see section 11).

6. What We Do Not Log

The API is deliberately built so that it does not write the following to its logs:

  • client IP addresses
  • email addresses
  • Apple identity tokens
  • session tokens
  • text read from your photos (OCR text)
  • photo object keys and presigned URLs

Operational logging is limited to what is needed to run and troubleshoot the service.

7. Recall Information (CPSC)

Hearth compares the items you save — by brand and category — against the recall database of the US Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC). That database is public US government data. No information about you or your items is sent to the CPSC: the matching runs entirely on our server.

Recall matches are best-effort. A match is not a determination that your specific unit has been recalled, and recall data may be incomplete, delayed, or out of date. Always check the official recall notice at https://www.saferproducts.gov before acting on anything the app shows you.

8. Data Retention

Your account record, items, photos, maintenance tasks, repair logs, push tokens, and the encrypted Apple refresh token remain stored until you delete your account. We keep no separate copy and no archive after deletion.

9. Deleting Your Account and Your Data

You can delete your account inside the app: open Settings, then tap Delete Account. Deletion hard-deletes your rows from our database and queues your photo objects for removal from Cloudflare R2. It also revokes the Apple sign-in grant using the refresh token described in section 2.1.

Deletion is immediate and irreversible. We cannot restore your data afterwards, and we cannot recover it for you.

You can also request deletion by emailing support@taomindapp.com.

10. Children's Privacy

Hearth is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Payments

Hearth offers optional paid plans: an auto-renewing subscription (monthly, quarterly, or yearly) and a one-time lifetime purchase. Every one of them is bought through the App Store, and Apple processes the payment. We never receive your card number, billing address, or any other payment detail, and we have no way to charge you directly — the App Store is the only route to a purchase. There is still no advertising anywhere in the app.

When you buy or restore a purchase, we do receive the App Store receipt for that transaction, and we use it for exactly one thing: to work out what your account is entitled to and record it against your account. A receipt identifies the product, the date of purchase, and a transaction identifier. It contains no payment details, and we do not use it for advertising or analytics.

What each plan includes, how renewal and cancellation work, and how refunds are handled are all set out in section 3 of our Terms of Service.

12. Your Choices

  • Delete everything: Settings, then Delete Account — see section 9.
  • Turn reminders off: in the app's notification settings, or in iOS Settings under Notifications.
  • Ask us: email support@taomindapp.com with any question about what is stored about you, or to ask us to delete it.
